`light.mp3` is peak-normalised at about -1.5 dBFS (about -21 LUFS: a very short, spiky sound cannot reach -14 LUFS under a -1 dBTP ceiling).
`crowd.mp3` is normalised to -16 LUFS (peak about -9 dB), with a 40 ms fade in and a 300 ms fade out.

The four game recordings (`bat`, `cheer`, `ooh`, `murmur`) are made from freesound's public HQ preview MP3s (the
originals need a login), not from the original files.
`bat.mp3` is peak-normalised at about -1.5 dBFS, with a 5 ms fade in and an 80 ms fade out.
`cheer.mp3` is normalised to -16 LUFS, with a 40 ms fade in and a 500 ms fade out.
`ooh.mp3` is normalised to -16 LUFS, with a 150 ms fade in and a 350 ms fade out.
`murmur.mp3` is normalised to -24 LUFS (deliberately soft) and loops seamlessly: its end is crossfaded into its start over 1.5 s.

The four tennis recordings (`hit`, `court`, `applause`, `hush`) were picked by Yash by ear and are also made from the HQ preview MP3s.
`hit.mp3` and `court.mp3` are peak-normalised at about -1.5 dBFS, with a 3 ms fade in and a fade out of 80 ms (`hit`) and 100 ms (`court`).
`applause.mp3` is normalised to -16 LUFS, with a 100 ms fade in and a 500 ms fade out.
`hush.mp3` is normalised to -24 LUFS and loops seamlessly: its end is crossfaded into its start over 1.5 s. It was lifted about 17 dB, so it has some tape hiss; it plays at a low level (0.3).
